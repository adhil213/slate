The Experience of Slate Writing

Before digital screens, students wrote on dark stone or slate boards using pieces of chalk. The tactile feeling of chalk dragging across a rough surface was a universal school experience. Digital Slate recreates this feeling in your browser.

How It Works

Open the app and start writing immediately. Use your finger on a touchscreen, a mouse on a desktop, or a stylus on a tablet. The chalk follows your movement with subtle sound and texture that mimics real chalk on slate.

Use Cases

  • Handwriting practice — practice letters and words with chalk
  • Kids' doodling — let children draw and write freely
  • Quick notes — jot down ideas with a chalk feel
  • Nostalgia — relive the school slate experience
  • Teaching — demonstrate writing in a classic format

Chalk Colors

Choose from four colors: classic white, bold red, soft rose, and warm orange. Each color writes with the same chalk-like texture and sound.
