A Slate That Fits in Your Pocket

Digital Slate is designed mobile-first. Open it on your Android or iPhone, and you get a full-screen writing surface that responds to your finger. No app to install, no account to create — just open and write.

Touch-Optimized Writing

The writing engine is built specifically for touch input. Your finger becomes the chalk. The slate responds immediately with no lag, even on older phones with limited RAM.

Works Offline

After your first visit, the slate works offline. No internet connection needed to write, erase, or clear. Your drawings are saved in your browser.

Phone-Friendly Features

  • Full-screen writing area
  • No browser scrolling while writing
  • Touch-optimized controls
  • Lightweight — works on low-end phones
  • Saves your work locally
  • Subtle chalk sound on touch

Who Uses a Slate on Their Phone?

  • Parents giving kids a quick drawing surface

  • Anyone who enjoys the chalk-on-slate feeling
  • People who want a simple, calm writing tool
