What is a Digital Slate?

A digital slate is a browser-based recreation of a traditional school writing slate. Before tablets and whiteboards, students wrote on dark stone slates using chalk. A digital slate brings that same experience to your phone, tablet, or computer — no download needed.

How to Use

  • Touch users: Write directly on the slate with your finger
  • Desktop users: Click and drag with your mouse to write
  • Stylus users: Use your stylus for natural writing

Choose from four chalk colors — white, red, rose, and orange. Use the eraser to rub out mistakes, or clear the entire slate with one tap.

Why a Digital Slate?

Whether you want to practice handwriting, let your kids doodle, take quick chalk-style notes, or simply enjoy the nostalgic feeling of writing on a slate — this tool is built for that. It's free, instant, and works entirely in your browser.

Features

  • Instant writing — no loading, no login
  • Finger, mouse, and stylus support
  • Chalk-like writing with subtle sound
  • Four chalk colors
  • Eraser tool
  • One-click clear
  • Works offline after first visit
  • Mobile-friendly design
  • Lightweight and fast
